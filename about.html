WHY MEDCORE

Why Healthcare Providers Choose Us

The reasons thousands of hospitals, distributors, and governments partner with MedCore — in their own words.

QualityUncompromising GMP-certified manufacturing

All three of our production facilities operate under WHO Good Manufacturing Practice guidelines with zero major regulatory recalls in the past five years. Every batch undergoes 14-stage quality testing before release — from raw material identity to finished product stability.

InnovationAI-driven drug discovery since 2021

Our proprietary AI platform has reduced drug discovery timelines by 42% and shortlisted 7 new molecular candidates for clinical trials since launch. We hold 214 active patents and consistently invest 12–14% of annual revenue back into R&D.

Reliability99.4% on-time delivery across 50+ countries

Our cold-chain logistics network and real-time serialisation system ensures that medicines reach hospitals and pharmacies on schedule. We operate dedicated distribution hubs in 12 countries with 24/7 supply chain monitoring.

ComplianceApproved in 6 major regulatory jurisdictions

MedCore holds active approvals from the US FDA, European Medicines Agency, CDSCO, TGA (Australia), Health Canada, and PMDA (Japan). Our regulatory team maintains a 94% first-cycle approval rate — among the highest in the industry.

SustainabilityCarbon-neutral target by 2028

We've reduced manufacturing emissions by 38% since 2019 through renewable energy installations and process optimisation. Our EcoVadis Gold Rating places us in the top 5% of pharmaceutical companies globally for environmental and social governance.
