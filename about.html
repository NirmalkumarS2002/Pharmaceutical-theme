Our Journey

25 Years of Breakthroughs

From a small research lab in Chennai to a global pharmaceutical leader — our story is written in milestones.

Founded in Chennai

Dr. Ananya Krishnan establishes MedCore from a 3-person research team, focused on affordable cardiovascular drugs for the Indian market.

1998 Founding

First FDA Approval

CardioCore-5 receives US FDA approval — our first internationally approved molecule, opening markets across North America and Europe.

2006 Global Expansion

Oncology Division Launch

Expansion into targeted cancer therapies with a ₹800 crore R&D investment. First oncology pipeline candidate enters Phase II trials.

2013 New Vertical

200th Medicine Milestone

OncoClear 100mg becomes our 200th approved medicine — marking a decade of growth from 42 products to a full therapeutic portfolio.

2023 Portfolio Milestone
