FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions before you reach out.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all general inquiries within one business day. Medical and safety-related reports are prioritized and typically addressed within a few hours.

Can I request product samples or documentation?

Yes, healthcare professionals and licensed distributors can request samples and technical documentation through our Sales & Partnerships team.

How do I report a side effect or adverse event?

Please email our Adverse Event Reporting team directly or call our 24/7 emergency helpline. All reports are handled confidentially and reviewed by our safety team.

Do you offer career opportunities for fresh graduates?

Yes, we run internship and graduate trainee programs across R&D, manufacturing, and quality assurance. Visit our Careers page for current openings.
